Snapshots, very large PDFs and DWG drawings.

- Snapshot and compare: keep a snapshot of the project, then ask what changed. Materials, structures, loads, water levels and phases are compared property by property, with before and after, and every change goes to a CSV file.
- Very large PDFs: a report past 100 pages or 18 MB is split by itself when you attach it. Each part is read whole, text and drawings, and answers come with the page numbers of the original.
- DWG drawings are read directly, with blocks opened up into their own lines. Drawings can be given back as DWG as well as DXF.
- The self-test now checks DWG and long PDFs too, and lists three more steps to try by hand.

Tested against a stand-in Plaxis only. DWG files were tested with drawings written by the tool itself, not yet with drawings saved from AutoCAD.
